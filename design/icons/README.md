# Palang's UI icons

The icons in `data/icons/scalable/actions/` (`palang-…-symbolic.svg`) replace
the Adwaita icons Palang used before (2026-10-22). They follow the logo's
style: 16×16, filled shapes, corners cut at 45° instead of rounded, and one
amber detail per icon.

- `1-sample-sheet.png`: the first five, next to the Adwaita icons they
  replace.
- `2-all-icons.png`: the whole set on dark (32 and 16 px) and light (16 px).
- `3-in-the-app.png`, `3-in-the-app-tree.png`: screenshots of the real app
  with them (virtual display).
- `render-sheet.sh`: makes that sheet again after a change.

How the icons are drawn (see `data/icons/README.md`).
